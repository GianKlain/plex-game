How to Plex

Each player starts with 12 stones, either yellow or blue. The red stone is in the centre of the game. Yellow places first.

Your turn

Place one stone

Take a stone from your row, turn it any way you like and lay it against the pattern. It must lie free and touch what is already there with at least two corners. Stones on the table are never moved.

Rule number one

Surround the red stone to win

Whoever can surround the red centre stone with their own colour wins the game, at once. Stones of your opponent may sit inside your ring.

Rule number two

Winning by points

To make points you need to make a hollow space, which you then fill with your own point stones. One triangle is one point, a diamond is two. The orange points belong to yellow, the violet points to blue. The bigger the hollow space, the more points you make.

Watch out: point stones are common ground. Your opponent can use them to surround the red stone.

If all stones are placed and nobody has surrounded the red stone, the player with more points wins. Equal points is a draw. Surrounding the red stone always beats a win on points.

Rule number three

Eating your opponent

If you can surround a stone of your opponent, you take it out of the game, change its colour to your own and add it to your row to use later.

The empty space that remains stays empty until the end of the game. It is common ground, like the point stones: it can help surround the red stone or more of your opponent's stones.

A ring needs at least one stone of the player who attacks. A stone that is closed in by common ground alone stays where it is, an isolated island.

Be careful with your own points. You can landlock your own stones with them, and then your opponent takes those.
